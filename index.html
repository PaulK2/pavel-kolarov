Project · Collectible network

Glass Trading

A physical collectible network where every piece carries its own encrypted identity.

Encrypted keysLocation-basedCollectibleCommerce

What makes Glass different is how each physical coin is tied to a verifiable digital identity. Every piece ships with its own individual encrypted registration key, so no two collectibles are interchangeable and ownership can be proven.

Activation is location-based: a coin is brought online where it's registered, and a public score / price interface lets any piece be looked up and tracked. The physical object and the network around it were designed as one system.
